Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Mohammed Reqasse ("I", "me", or "my") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit my portfolio website at moreqasse.github.io/MyCV/ (the "Site").

Information I Collect

Contact Form: When you submit the contact form, I collect your name, email address, subject, and message. This information is sent directly to my email address and is not stored on any server.

Automated Data: Like most websites, the Site automatically collects certain information when you visit, including:

  • IP address (anonymized by GitHub Pages)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL
  • Pages viewed and time spent on each page

This data is collected and processed by GitHub Pages as my hosting provider. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for details on how they handle this data.

How I Use Your Information

I use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your inquiries sent through the contact form

Cookies

The Site uses a single functional cookie to store your preferred language setting. No tracking or analytics cookies are used.

Third-Party Services

  • GitHub Pages: Hosting provider. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google reCAPTCHA: Used on the contact form to prevent spam. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: Used for typography. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained in my email inbox until the purpose for which they were collected has been fulfilled, or as required by law.

Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me at reqasse@gmail.com.

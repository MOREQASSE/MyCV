Humanity at the heart of our care

Humanism at the heart of our care

Humanism is simply respect for dignity, solidarity and kindness. That is exactly our association’s DNA. Since 1998, volunteers, caregivers and donors take turns offering presence, care and comfort to isolated elders in Taourirt and surrounding douars.

We intervene at home, in partnership with the local health centre, and run a warm welcoming place where intergenerational bonds are woven. Each visit, each meal, each smile counts.

Our vision

A city where no elder feels forgotten, where each older person is surrounded, listened to and respected.

Our promise

Our history

From 1998 to today, continuity.

An association born from civic momentum, structured over the years without ever losing its soul: proximity.

98 1998 — Foundation

A few families in Taourirt gather to visit and support isolated elders.

05 2005 — First premises

Opening of the administrative premises and launch of weekly solidarity meals.

15 2015 — Health

Partnership with the health centre: screenings and nursing follow-up for our beneficiaries.

24 2024 — 1,200 households

Over 14,400 meals and 340 home visits, thanks to 45 volunteers and donors.

Our approach

Intervene quickly, follow long-term.

One report is enough. Our method is simple, human and rigorous — designed with families.

1

Report & listen

Form or call. Acknowledged within 24h, in full confidentiality.

2

Visit & assessment

Volunteer pair on site, with the family and the health center if needed.

3

Long-term support

Aid plan, regular visits, meals, intergenerational bonds — support over time.
